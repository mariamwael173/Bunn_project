Our story

From the farm to your cup, with nothing in between.

Bunn started with a simple idea: coffee should taste like where it came from. We buy directly from small farms, roast in small batches, and serve every cup within days of roasting.

Whether you stop by for a quick espresso or stay for the afternoon, we want the table to feel like yours.
